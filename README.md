# Kelly's Giraffe Getaway

A free retro side-scrolling platformer from Kelly's Arcade / Peter's Arcade.
Cackles the Hyena King captured Gigi the giraffe! Kelly walks Level 1 on foot, frees Jinxy (a young giraffe, smaller than Gigi, with a short neck and brown patches), then rides her through 8 more levels: neck RAM (aimable), neck-bite SWING from hooks and ledges, tongue snacks, and green gems. Cackles gets a furious second phase.

Beat the game to unlock 2 BONUS rounds (saved in localStorage): Peter has been captured! Bonus 1, The Big Chase, follows the kidnappers' wagon; Bonus 2, Tusk's Fortress, is a falling-rock gauntlet and a fight with Big Tusk the Warthog, ending on the PETER IS SAVED! screen.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/kellys-giraffe-getaway/
